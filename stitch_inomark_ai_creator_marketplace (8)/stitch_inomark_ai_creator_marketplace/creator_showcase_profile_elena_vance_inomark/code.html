Director Profile

About Elena Vance

With over 8 years of classical cinematic commercial directing experience in Munich and Paris, Elena transitioned her studio focus toward deterministic AI workflows in 2023. Rather than treating generative models as slot machines, she architects rigorous node-based pipelines where prompt semantics, LoRA weights, and physical lighting models can be dialed with clinical precision.

Her philosophy unites the tactile sensuality of high-fashion optics — shallow depth of field, anamorphic bokeh, and subsurface scattering — with cutting-edge diffusion architecture. The result is visual output that enterprise creative directors can deploy straight to global commercial campaigns without the synthetic artifacts commonly associated with generative media.

CASE STUDY Nordic Botanical Serum
00:14 / 00:30 · 4K ProRes Master 24.000 FPS

The Creative Concept & Generative Pipeline

The objective was to visualize the microscopic bio-affinity of cold-pressed sea buckthorn and lingonberry seed extracts without the traditional expense and physical mess of high-speed Phantom macro shoots.

Elena developed a proprietary LoRA fine-tuned on optical caustics through laboratory-grade silica glass. Seed sequences were generated via a multi-pass ComfyUI graph to enforce deterministic fluid viscosity, followed by temporal upsampling via Runway Gen-3 Alpha to produce consistent fluid surface tension.
