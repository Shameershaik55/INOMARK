✦ Answers & Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining Inomark as a creator or hiring as a brand.

1. What is Inomark? expand_more

Inomark is a specialized creative talent marketplace designed specifically for the AI era. We connect verified AI content creators, prompt engineers, and visualizers with global brands, agencies, and studios seeking custom generative visuals and commercial campaigns.

2. Who can join? expand_more

Anyone creating exceptional digital work with generative AI (filmmakers, 3D artists, concept illustrators) can apply to join as a creator. Brands, agencies, marketing teams, and startup founders can create brand accounts to discover talent and publish briefs.

3. Can creators showcase AI videos and animations? expand_more

Yes! Inomark natively hosts high-definition video loops, MP4 clips, and character animations generated using Runway Gen-3, Luma Dream Machine, Kling, Pika, Stable Video, and traditional compositing tools.

4. Which details can creators add to their profiles? expand_more

Creators can specify their toolchains (e.g. Midjourney, Flux, ComfyUI, Blender), primary aesthetic styles, commercial experience, turnaround times, LoRA specialization, prompt philosophies, and direct portfolio case studies.

5. How do brands find creators with specific skills? expand_more

Brands can use our multi-tier search filters to sort creators by model expertise, style genre (photorealism, stylized 3D, surreal fashion), deliverable format (video vs stills), and client review ratings.

6. What is a campaign brief? expand_more

A campaign brief is a standardized project outline posted by a brand. It sets forth deliverables (asset count, aspect ratios, resolution), mood reference materials, timeline milestones, budget ranges, and commercial licensing buyouts.

7. Can brands save creators? expand_more

Yes. Brands and agency accounts can bookmark creators into custom collections and private talent rosters (e.g., “Q4 Holiday Campaign”, “Automotive Concept Specs”) for fast hiring.

8. Can creators explore brand opportunities? expand_more

Yes. Creators can browse the public and invite-only Campaign Brief board to pitch on campaigns matching their tools, aesthetic style, and budget preferences.

9. Do I need an account to use the full marketplace? expand_more

Anyone can browse our public gallery previews. However, submitting proposals, contacting artists, accessing full portfolio metadata, and commissioning briefs requires a free Inomark account.

10. How do I join as a creator or brand? expand_more

Simply click "Get Started" in the header or select "Join as a Creator" / "Hire Creators" anywhere on this page. Choose your account role and you can configure your profile in under 2 minutes.
